# About

InjazApps builds mobile apps that do one thing properly.

## How it works

Small on purpose. Every app is designed, built, and supported by the same
hands. That means fewer apps and slower releases than a larger team would
manage. It also means the person who wrote the code answers the support
email.

## What every app follows

Apps work offline. Network access is a feature, not a requirement.

No account unless the app genuinely needs one.

Your files stay on your device unless you move them.

## The apps

Each app is its own product, with its own name and its own icon. They are
not sold as a suite and they do not depend on each other. OwlMD is
released. The others are still being built.

## The mark

The company mark is a joggled head joint. In stone masonry, a mason cuts
the vertical joint between two upright stones into interlocking keys. The
joint resists sliding, and a flat span behaves like an arch. Two pieces,
cut to fit, each holding the other up — which is the only claim this
company makes about its own work.

## Where the geometry comes from

The forms are taken from Mamluk-era architecture in Cairo, fourteenth and
fifteenth century stonework. The vocabulary is structural, not decorative:
ablaq, the alternating courses of light and dark stone; the joggled joint;
the rank blazon. What is borrowed is the structure of a form, never its
surface.

## The colours

Limestone and basalt are the two stones. The red comes from striped
façades. The teal is not from tilework — Mamluk Cairo was not a glazed-tile
culture. It comes from enamelled glass mosque lamps. The brass comes from
inlaid metalwork, and appears once on a screen, never twice.
